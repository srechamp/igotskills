# Market signals behind coffee-chat (researched 2026-10-06)

Purpose: justify why referral outreach is Section 1 and why Sections 2-4 are ordered as they are. Figures are directional, come from secondary sources of uneven quality, and move. **Re-run a web search before quoting any number to the user or putting one in a message** (project research rule). None of these stats belong in the outreach message itself.

## Referrals and cold applications
- Pragmatic Engineer (Jul 7, 2026), interviews with 50+ hiring managers and engineers: experienced engineers report getting interviews mainly through people they know; larger companies use AI to sift resumes they're "bombarded" with. Hot market for AI/ML/FDE roles, tough for most others. https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/tech-jobs-market-in-2026-part-3-hiring
- Job-search statistics roundup (2026 aggregations): referrals estimated at 30-50% of hires while being about 7% of applicants. Aggregated, not primary data. https://airesume.guru/blog/job-search-statistics-2026
- Clutch (Jul 28, 2026): applicant pools keep growing; about 30% of tech workers submit 51-75 applications before landing; companies take referrals seriously. https://clutch.co/resources/how-get-tech-job-2026
- Lower-confidence claims seen (4x offer likelihood for referrals; 7x interview odds): vendor blogs citing other sources. Don't repeat as fact.
- The unsourced "under 1% conversion" claim in the original Gemini notes was not verified. Don't use it.

## What hiring wants now (drives Section 2)
- Fastest-growing SRE requirements for 2026: platform engineering, FinOps, AIOps; incident-management leadership rated as the top soft skill. https://resumegeni.com/skills-guides/site-reliability-engineer
- Platform engineering demand: Gartner projection of 80% of large engineering orgs with platform teams by end of 2026 (up from 45% in 2022), cited across recruiter sources; interviews now test IDP design and adoption thinking. https://www.kore1.com/platform-engineer-interview-questions/ and https://www.kore1.com/how-to-hire-platform-engineer-2026/
- Dice figures via Clutch: tech postings up 27% YoY in June 2026; 75% of tech postings include AI-related skills.

## Where demand is growing (drives Section 3)
- TechServe Alliance, Jan-Jun 2026 vs 2025 tech hiring growth by sector: Insurance +69%, Consulting +34%, Manufacturing +33%, Finance/Banking +29%, Aerospace/Defense +27%, Software +18%, Healthcare +7%, Retail -3%. https://techservealliance.org/?p=84569
- Recruiter-side source: largest hiring-volume growth in regulated enterprises (banking, insurance, healthcare). https://kitalent.com/ai-technology-and-digital-infrastructure-recruitment/cloud-and-platform-recruitment/platform-engineering-recruitment/platform-engineer-recruitment

## Why proof-of-work still matters (Section 4)
- Hiring managers report AI-enhanced resumes read as impressive but disappoint in practice, and fake candidates are a concern (Pragmatic Engineer, above). Inspectable artifacts reduce that risk for the reader.
- 92% of surveyed tech professionals believe AI screening misses qualified candidates (TechServe, above), which is the opening for human-to-human outreach.
