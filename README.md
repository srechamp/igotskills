<h1 align="center">Skills For A Better Job Hunt</h1>

<p align="center"><em>What are these skills, and do they actually help you improve your job search and reach an offer letter?</em></p>

---

## What's in this repo

| Skill | Folder | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Full-time employment (FTE) analysis** | [`fte-role-analysis/`](fte-role-analysis/) | Evaluates one to five full-time postings at once: go/no-go, gap analysis, ranking, a tailored resume, interview prep and a 30/60/90-day plan. |
| **Contract role analysis** | [`contract-role-analysis/`](contract-role-analysis/) | Vets a contract role submitted through a staffing agency: legitimacy check on each layer, requirements match, resume alignment, interview questions and a short ramp-up plan. |
| **Coffee chat (the warm channel)** | [`coffee-chat/`](coffee-chat/) | Drafts a natural email or LinkedIn message asking engineering leadership for a 15-minute virtual coffee chat, plus the follow-up nudge and thank-you. |

## How to use them

Drop these skills into your job search thread or job hunt project space on any AI platform to get the most out of your search. Each folder holds a `SKILL.md` with the full instructions.
